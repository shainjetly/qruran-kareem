Privacy policy

Qur'an Kareem · Last updated 16 October 2026

Short version: the app collects no personal data.

No account, no tracking

You never sign in or give your name, email or phone number. The app has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Your data stays on your phone

Bookmarks, reading progress, streaks, badges, recent searches and your settings are saved on your device. The app never sends them to us or anyone else. If your phone's backup is turned on (iCloud or Google), your phone may include them in that backup, as it does for other apps. Deleting the app deletes them from the phone.

What the app downloads

The app uses our content server (hosted on Cloudflare) to:

  • check for updated content lists each time it starts, and when you open the translation or language choices;
  • refresh the list of available recitations;
  • download translations, tafsir, word meanings, word audio, word timings and Mushaf page fonts when you open or download them;
  • play or download recitation audio.

Each request only names the file wanted; the app adds no identifier. Like any web server, the server sees your internet address and basic connection details so it can send the file. We do not use them to identify you. Downloads stay on your phone and can be removed in Settings → Downloads.

Location (Qibla and prayer times)

If you allow it, the Qibla compass and prayer times use your approximate location to work out the direction of the Kaaba and the times of prayer. The location is used on your phone only, when you open them or when the app sets your prayer reminders: it is never sent to us or anyone else, and never saved. If you choose a city or island instead, its name is saved on your phone. Both work without location if you choose a city. Prayer reminders are notifications set on your phone; nothing about them leaves it.

Contributions

If you choose to support the app, the payment is made through the App Store or Google Play. Qur'an Kareem never sees your name, card or payment details; it only learns that a contribution was completed, to say thank you, and keeps that on your phone. A contribution unlocks nothing.

Sharing

When you copy or share an ayah, the text goes only where you choose to send it, through your phone's own share menu.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
